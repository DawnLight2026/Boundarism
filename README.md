# Boundarism
### A Guidance for the AI Era

Human civilization collapses when boundaries disappear.

Throughout history, political conflicts, ideological struggles, and institutional failures often share the same hidden structural flaw: the disappearance of clear boundaries between power, responsibility, and meaning.

**Boundarism** is a philosophical framework for the AI era.  
It explores how civilizations maintain stability through clear definitions, limited goals, and enforceable failure conditions.

The core principles are simple:

- Equality means **equal rules of interpretation**, not equal outcomes.
- Freedom exists only within **a defined set of choices**.
- Every goal must have **a clear failure condition**.
- Systems without boundaries inevitably drift toward chaos or domination.

As artificial intelligence increasingly participates in decision-making and social organization, the importance of boundary-based thinking becomes even more critical.

This book is not a political manifesto.

It is an attempt to rediscover a structural truth that may have existed for thousands of years but was gradually forgotten.

If civilization survives the AI era, it will not be because humans became wiser.

It will be because we finally remembered where the boundaries are.
